## Method

- **Eligible:** choice questions with ≥ 3 options; all other questions keep the identity read.
- **Gate:** identity top probability at the shipped `header` choice T (4.263) < τ, τ ∈ {0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9}
  (the gated-read thresholds of option-ordering §7). Re-reads collected once at τ 0.9; smaller τ are subsets.
- **Arms:** keep the first read's top 2; top 3 (n > 3 only); top ⌈n/2⌉; `halving` = keep ⌈m/2⌉ and re-read while
  the latest read's top probability < τ and m > 2.
- **Re-read:** the kept options in their original order, relabelled A, B, …, one identity read. The prompt is what
  production would send: same row, header question list with all of the row's questions, same profile padding;
  only the re-asked question's options shrink (its shared prefix was asserted equal to the original's).
- **Final distribution:** kept options get the re-read's probabilities scaled by the previous read's mass on them;
  dropped options keep their previous probabilities.
- **Scoring:** index-dev-2k against identity on the same questions, 95% paired bootstrap. T_id (identity, calib-600)
  and T_cut (per arm, on calib-600 re-reads) refit; accuracy does not depend on T.
