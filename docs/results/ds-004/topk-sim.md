# DS-004 step 0: offline top-K simulation

What a `logprobs=K` readout (the fallback when MTP is on) would do to shipped answers,
simulated on CPU from the collected full label reads. Plan:
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-06-ds-004-mtp-spike.md`, step 0.

- **Data:** `.cache/collect/<profile>/eval.jsonl.gz`, 2,827 gold-labelled questions per
  profile (1,607 choice, 554 score, 666 noul); 12 rows the collect refused are left out.
- **Pipeline:** each profile's shipped settings (`header` layout, `identity` orders), its
  `data/<profile>/header/calibration.json` (stamp checked against the collect), then
  `scripts.offline.questions` / `scored_one` → `gateway.decide.probabilities`. The full
  read and the truncated read go through the same code; only the label logprobs differ.
- **Truncation:** a label is kept if it is among the K highest labels and its logprob is
  at least θ (the assumed logprob of the K-th vocab token). When more than K labels sit
  above θ they fill the K slots, so θ is raised to the K-th label's logprob.
  - `upper` fill: missing labels at θ (true value is at most this);
  - `lower` fill: missing labels at `-inf` (exact in `softmax`: weight 0).
  - No question lost every label at any setting, so no uniform fallback was needed.
- **Metrics:** `|dp|` is the largest change over a question's options in the returned
  probabilities. `p99` is over all questions, `mean` over affected ones. "Δlevel" is the
  change in the shipped score-type `score` (expected level).
- **Run:** `uv run python scripts/spike_topk_sim.py` (about 20 s). Full tables, every K, θ
  and type: [Full tables](#full-tables) below.

## What this means

- **No answer changes under the shipped settings, by construction.** With one option
  order and the top label always kept, both fills leave the top label on top (upper puts
  missing labels at or below every kept one). A noul "yes" at p ≥ 0.5 stays at p ≥ 0.5
  the same way. So flips and accuracy are 0 here; only probabilities move: choice
  `confidence` and `probabilities`, score `score` and `confidence`, noul `noul`.
  Averaged option orders (`identity+reversed`, `cyclic:k`) could flip; not simulated.
- **θ matters more than K.** K only binds on the 7.4-10.7% of questions with more than
  K labels. Below that, rows are set by θ, the K-th vocab logprob.
- **Gemma is the worst case**, as the plan expected. Its labels sit far below the top
  (median lowest label -22.8), and its high T (4.3-7.6) turns a filled-in tail into real
  mass. Even at θ = -20 the upper/lower bracket on max |dp| is 0.60 / 0.30.
- **Qwen at K = 64 with θ ≤ -15** stays within max |dp| 0.071 (3.6) and 0.027 (3.8)
  for either fill, p99 ≤ 0.005. At K = 20 the >20-label choice questions alone give
  0.30 / 0.08 (3.6) and 0.15 / 0.06 (3.8).
- **For scale:** bf16 load noise is about 0.03/T, and the stability test warns above 0.05.
- **The bracket is wide where it matters.** The upper and lower fills differ by about
  5x on Qwen3.6 at θ = -8 (0.63 vs 0.12). Where the real answer falls depends on the true
  tail, which only a real top-K read shows.

## Caveats

- **θ is unknown.** The real K-th vocab logprob per row, and so which labels a top-K read
  returns, is measured only in step 2 (arm B). Collect stored label logprobs only, so a
  label's vocab rank is unknown here: K counts labels, not vocab tokens. Non-label tokens
  above a label push it out sooner than this simulation does.
- **The fill rule is not chosen yet.** The upper and lower fills bracket any rule that
  puts missing labels between -inf and θ. A real readout would know θ per row (the K-th
  returned logprob), which this simulation assumes.
- **One θ for every row.** A real read has a different θ per row and per question.
- **Eval split only, identity orders only.** The calibration was fitted on the full
  reads and is reused unchanged, as the gateway would.
